### Phase 3 top idea

**App:** One Transit — daily iOS widget showing **one actionable transit + one sentence**, nothing else.

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Score | 78/100 |
| MRR band | $10k–50k Estimated |
| Competitors | Co-Star (4.5★, cluttered), Sanctuary ($10/mo), The Pattern |

## Example C — Phase 6 closing (any run)

### Devil's advocate (5 reasons this fails)

1. Astrology market is saturated; users may not switch from Co-Star without marketing spend.
2. Widget-only apps have lower in-app conversion — users never open the app to hit paywall.
3. Transit accuracy expectations may trigger bad reviews if copy feels generic.
4. Seasonal churn during non-belief periods (user deletes after breakup with ex who introduced app).
5. App Store may classify as entertainment — hard to justify $4.99/mo without depth.

### Research homework

1. Manually read 50 recent 1-star Co-Star reviews — confirm "anxiety" and "too much" themes.
2. Search App Store "astrology widget" — count competitors, note pricing.
3. Post a one-line concept in r/astrology (value-first, not promotional) — gauge reaction.

### Recommendation

**Research more** — validate widget conversion path before 4-week build. If 3/5 homework items confirm wedge, **ship MVP**.
