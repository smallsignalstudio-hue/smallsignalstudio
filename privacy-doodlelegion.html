Privacy Policy

Doodle Legion by Small Signal Studio · Effective 21 November 2026
Live URL: https://smallsignalstudio.com/privacy-doodlelegion.html

This Privacy Policy explains how Small Signal Studio (“we”, “us”) handles information in connection with Doodle Legion, a lane-battle game for iOS in which you draw your own Captain and lead a doodle army.

1. Who we are

Controller: Small Signal Studio.
Contact: hello@smallsignalstudio.com or our Doodle Legion Support page.

2. The short version

  • Doodle Legion has no account and we run no game server. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number or precise location.
  • Your progress, settings and Captain drawing are stored on your device (and, if you use Game Center, in your own iCloud).
  • Third-party services we use for ads, analytics, game configuration and purchases collect some device and usage data, described below. They identify you only by random or device identifiers, never by name.
  • The only data used for tracking is your device’s advertising identifier, and only if you allow it in the iOS tracking prompt.

3. Data stored on your device

The game saves the following on your device only:

  • game progress: stages, stars, units, cards, coins, gems, missions and Daily Doodle history;
  • settings such as sound, haptics, language and screen shake;
  • your Captain drawing and the Captain name you type (a word filter blocks offensive names);
  • an age band (under 13, 13–15 or 16+) from the first-launch age question. We do not store your birth year;
  • a random install ID created by the game, used as the analytics user ID (see section 6).

We do not receive this data. Your Captain drawing only leaves your device when you share a result card yourself (section 9).

4. Game Center and iCloud (Apple)

If you are signed in to Game Center, the game uses it for leaderboards (including the Daily Doodle), achievements, friends’ times and a backup of your progress stored as a Game Center saved game in your iCloud. This data is handled by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy; we do not receive it. Your Game Center nickname is shown to other players on leaderboards according to your Game Center settings.

5. Ads (Google AdMob)

Doodle Legion shows rewarded ads only, and only when you choose to watch one for a reward. There are no banners and no forced ads. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect:

  • device identifiers — the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking in the iOS prompt, plus other device-level identifiers;
  • coarse location derived from your IP address (the game never asks for your location);
  • ad interactions (which ads were shown, watched or tapped);
  • performance and diagnostic data (for example load times and crashes).

Google uses this to serve and measure ads, prevent fraud and, where you allow it, personalize ads. See How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Consent. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent through Google’s User Messaging Platform before personalized ads. You can change your choice any time in Settings → Privacy choices. On iOS, the system tracking prompt appears (after your first battle, players 13 and older only); you can change it later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. If you decline, you still get ads and rewards, just not personalized ones.

6. Analytics (GameAnalytics)

We use GameAnalytics to understand how the game is played and to fix problems. It receives, under the random install ID from section 3:

  • gameplay events (for example stage started, won or lost, cards picked, ads watched);
  • purchase events (product, price and currency — never payment details);
  • device model, OS version, app version, performance and crash data.

See the GameAnalytics Privacy Policy.

7. Game configuration (Unity)

The game downloads balance and offer settings from Unity Remote Config. To do that it signs in anonymously with Unity Authentication, which creates a random player ID. Unity may also receive coarse location derived from your IP address and basic diagnostic data, and the Unity engine sends anonymous hardware statistics. See the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

8. Purchases (Apple and RevenueCat)

In-app purchases are sold and charged by Apple. We use RevenueCat to check which purchases you own; it receives your purchase history with an anonymous ID. Neither we nor RevenueCat receive your payment card details. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

9. Sharing

When you tap Share or Copy result card, the game hands a result image or text (which can include your Captain drawing, Captain name and battle result) to the iOS share sheet or clipboard. Where it goes is your choice; it is not sent to us. Links in shared cards open smallsignalstudio.com/doodle, a static page that sets no cookies.

10. Children

Doodle Legion is not directed at children. Because younger players may still play, the game asks for an age band on first launch. If the answer is under 13: the iOS tracking prompt is never shown, ad requests are marked as child-directed and non-personalized, the consent form is not shown, and no analytics user ID is set. Players aged 13–15 are treated as under the age of consent for ads. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

11. Legal bases (EEA/UK)

Personalized ads rely on your consent. Non-personalized ads, analytics, crash reporting and game configuration rely on our legitimate interests in running, funding, securing and improving the game. Purchases rely on performing the contract with you.

12. Retention and deletion

Settings → Delete local data erases your progress on the device and, if you are signed in to Game Center, your Game Center backup. Deleting the app also removes local data. Your purchases stay with your Apple ID and can be restored.

Data held by the services above is kept according to their own retention policies. Because we only see random IDs, we cannot look you up by name; if you want to exercise a right over that data, email us and we will help with the steps (for example sharing the version line from Settings). You can reset your advertising identifier in iOS Settings at any time.

13. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict personal data, to object to processing, to data portability, to withdraw consent and to complain to your local data-protection authority. We do not sell personal information for money. Sharing device data with Google for personalized ads happens only with your consent and tracking permission, and you can stop it through Privacy choices and the iOS tracking setting.

14. International transfers

Our providers may process data in the United States or other countries. Where required they rely on appropriate safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses) for transfers from the EEA, the UK and Switzerland.

15. Changes

We may update this policy, for example when we add or change a service. The effective date at the top will change, and material changes will be noted in the app’s update notes.

16. Contact

Privacy questions: hello@smallsignalstudio.com or Doodle Legion Support.
