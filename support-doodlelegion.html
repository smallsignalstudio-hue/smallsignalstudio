Bugs, purchase problems or questions — email us. We read every message. Please include the version line at the bottom of Settings (for example “Version 1.0.0 (12)”) and your device model. In the game, Settings → Support opens a new email for you.

Frequently asked

How do I keep my progress on a new device?

Sign in to Game Center (iOS Settings → Game Center) and turn on iCloud Drive. The game backs up your progress as a Game Center saved game and loads it on any device signed in to the same Apple ID. Without Game Center, progress stays on this device only.

My purchase did not unlock.

Open Settings or the Shop and tap Restore purchases while connected to the internet and signed in with the Apple ID you bought with. Gems you already spent cannot be restored. For refunds, use reportaproblem.apple.com.

What does the No-Ads Pass do?

There are no forced ads in Doodle Legion: every ad is optional and rewarded. With the No-Ads Pass you tap “Claim” and get the reward without watching (daily limits still apply), plus the extra Daily Doodle attempt each day.

An ad did not load. Did I lose my reward?

If an ad does not load within a few seconds, the game gives you the reward anyway, a few times per day. After that, try again later or on a better connection.

When does the Daily Doodle reset?

Every day at 00:00 UTC. Everyone plays the same battle with the same army that day. If the game says “Check your device date.”, set your device to set the date and time automatically.

How do I change ad privacy or tracking?

In the EEA and UK, use Settings → Privacy choices. On iOS you can change tracking any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Details are in our Privacy Policy.

How do I start over or delete my data?

Settings → Delete local data erases your progress on this device and, if you are signed in to Game Center, your Game Center backup. This cannot be undone. Purchases stay with your Apple ID and can be restored. We have no account or server copy of your data; for anything else, email hello@smallsignalstudio.com.

How do I change the language?

Settings → Language. “Device language” follows your iPhone or iPad language.
