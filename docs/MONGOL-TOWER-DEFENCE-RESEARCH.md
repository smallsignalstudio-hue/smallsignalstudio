# MONGOL EMPIRE POWER — Exhaustive Dual-Use Research Brief (Tower-Defence Game Bible)

> Dense notes for a **multi-ulūs campaign bible**.  
> **Not one timeless “horde skin.”** Separate **united empire under Chinggisids (1206–c. 1260)**, then **Yuan (China)**, **Ilkhanate (Iran/Iraq)**, **Chagatai** (Central Asia), and **Golden Horde / Jochid** (Pontic–Caspian steppe) as **fractured successor polities** with different religions, logistics, and enemies.  
> Companion style to `VIKING-TOWER-DEFENCE-RESEARCH.md` / `CHINESE-IMPERIAL-TOWER-DEFENCE-RESEARCH.md` / Persian / Egyptian / Maya–Aztec / Greek TD research (same project).

### Source classes (use throughout)
| Tag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **CHR** | Near-contemporary or near-generation chronicles: *Secret History of the Mongols* (SHM), Rashīd al-Dīn *Jāmiʿ al-tawārīkh*, Juwaynī *Tārīkh-i Jahāngushāy*, Chinese dynastic/Yuan materials (*Yuan shi* tradition), Latin letters (Carpini, Rubruck), Armenian/Georgian/Syriac notices — partisan, invaluable, not video |
| **ARCH** | Archaeology / material culture: camps, cities (Karakorum, Shangdu, Dadu), siege debris, horse gear, wall phases, shipwreck debates for Japan invasions |
| **EPIG / DOC** | Inscriptions, paiza / passport tablets, administrative documents, Persian/Chinese fiscal records |
| **MOD** | Modern scholarly consensus or live debate (Morgan, Allsen, Biran, Jackson, May, Di Cosmo, Rossabi, Atwood, Broadbridge, Weatherford popular vs academic split, etc.) |
| **FOLK** | Later legend, romantic “Mongol warrior” tourism, modern neo-Tengrism / nationalist reconstructions, movie Chinggis, cartoon “barbarian horde” — **abilities/skins yes; chronology no** |

**Legend vs history rule for design:** Prefer **CHR/ARCH/DOC** for “what happened.” Use *Secret History* as **political memory + oral epic craft**, not a GPS battle log. Use **FOLK** only as optional skin toggles (never silent chronology). Never treat 21st-c. neo-Tengrism as 13th-c. court religion.

**Naming rule:** Prefer **Chinggis / Chinggis Khan** (or **Genghis** once as English alias) for Temüjin after 1206; **Ögedei**, **Möngke**, **Hülegü**, **Qubilai / Kublai**; **Yuan** for the China-based dynasty; **Ilkhanate**, **Chagatai ulūs**, **Golden Horde / Jochid** for western/central successor states. “Mongol Empire” = united phase + contested claim of qaghan supremacy after fracture.

**Chronology note:** Campaign years often firm from Persian/Chinese synchronisms; troop numbers in Latin/Persian/Chinese texts are routinely inflated (**MOD** humility). Japan invasion meteorology (“kamikaze”) is real weather + **FOLK** later sacralisation — keep both layers labeled.

**Script / language note:** Mongolian names in scholarly transcription where useful (Činggis, Sübe’etei / Subutai, Ögödei); Persian and Chinese forms noted at first mention. Do not treat *Yuan shi* or Rashīd as court CCTV.

**Design takeaway:** Mongol strength is **united steppe command + adopted Chinese/Persian siege packages + decimal logistics + psychological warfare**. Weakness arrives when **qaghan succession** breaks the machine, when **water/jungle/naval theatres** deny horse logistics, and when **settled-dynasty sinicization / Islamisation** splits identity and elite incentives across ulūs.

## 2. STRONGEST PEAKS — Why strong

### 2.1 Chinggis unification & early conquest machine (1206–1227) — **United steppe war OS**
**Why strong**
- **Political reboot:** Quriltai legitimacy + destruction/reorganisation of rival tribal aristocracies; promotion by merit/loyalty (*nökör* bonds) over pure lineage in key commands (**CHR** SHM; **MOD**).
- **Decimal military OS:** Units of 10 / 100 / 1,000 / 10,000 (*tumen*) — scalable command, transferability, census logic later formalised (**CHR/MOD**).
- **Mobility doctrine:** Multiple remounts, hunting-*nerge* encirclement adapted to war, feigned retreat, simultaneous multi-column approaches (**CHR/MOD** May, Di Cosmo).
- **Intelligence & diplomacy-as-war:** Merchant networks, envoys, deliberate terror after resistance, selective clemency for surrender — not mindless slaughter as sole system (**MOD** nuance vs **CHR** atrocity rhetoric).
- **Opening sedentary school:** Xi Xia / Jin wars force early adoption of siege engineers, mass conscript labour, and Chinese specialists (**MOD** Allsen on technology transfer).

**Why ranked a founding peak:** Creates the **only** Eurasian force that can treat the steppe *and* walled cities as one operational continuum within a generation.

**Game tone:** Tribe-unification tech tree; decimal upgrade slots; “surrender now” card vs “resist and burn” escalation.

### 2.2 Ögedei–Subutai–Batu western thunder (1230s–1241) — **Operational projection peak**
**Why strong**
- **Staff excellence:** Subutai as theatre designer; Batu as Jochid political spearhead — combined arms of politics + operations (**CHR**).
- **Logistics maturity:** Pre-positioned herds, local auxiliaries, winter campaigning comfort relative to European foes (**MOD**).
- **Siege + open-field mix:** Rus’ towns fall; Hungarian plain battle (Mohi) shows bridge/river denial and multi-corps timing (**CHR**).
- **Strategic surprise:** Europe lacks institutional answers to Mongol operational art in 1241 (**MOD**).

**Why ranked a hard-power peak:** Deepest simultaneous shock into Latin Christendom; withdrawal is **political** (Ögedei’s death / succession gravity), not a field defeat in Hungary.

**Game tone:** Multi-map corps; river-bridge puzzles (Mohi); sudden “qaghan dead — return east” forced mission abort.

### 2.3 Möngke restoration + Hülegü’s Baghdad stroke (1251–1258) — **Second united peak**
**Why strong**
- **Admin reboot:** Möngke’s censuses, fiscal tightening, multi-front doctrine (China + West Asia) (**CHR**; Allsen **MOD**).
- **Specialist packages:** Chinese siege engineers, Persian administrators, multiethnic tumens — empire as **talent magnet** (**MOD** Allsen).
- **Symbolic kill:** End of Abbasid caliphate at Baghdad (1258) — civilizational shock that still must not be cartoon “Mongols vs Islam forever” (Ilkhanate later Islamises) (**CHR/MOD**).

**Why ranked a peak:** Last moment when a single qaghan can meaningfully orchestrate **China and Iran theatres** before Toluid civil war.

**Game tone:** Census buildings unlock army caps; specialist artisan cards; Baghdad as wonder-destruction mission with legitimacy blowback meter.

### 2.4 Kublai’s Yuan conquest of Song (1260s–1279) — **Siege-tech adoption max**
**Why strong**
- **Chinese theatre mastery:** Xiangyang–Fancheng long siege; naval/river war; incorporation of Song administrators and artisans (**CHR**).
- **Artillery hinge:** Counterweight trebuchets (often linked to West Asian specialists in **CHR/MOD** debate) — climax of “steppe + siege science” fusion.
- **Dynastic claim:** Yuan as *Chinese* conquest dynasty while retaining Mongol elite privileges — dual face (**MOD** Rossabi).

**Caveat:** This peak is **China-weighted**; western ulūs are already drifting. “Mongol Empire” map-paint unity is partly ideology after 1260 (**MOD**).

**Game tone:** River-fort marathon; artisan transfer from captured cities; “become Son of Heaven” prestige vs “stay steppe pure” faction split.

### Peak summary

| Peak | Strength type | Signature systems | Bias warning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chinggis 1206–27 | United steppe OS + early siege school | Decimal army, *nerge*, terror/clemency | SHM is epic memory |
| Ögedei–Batu–Subutai 1236–41 | Eurasian operational projection | Multi-corps, winter war, Mohi timing | Europe “saved by death” is politics |
| Möngke–Hülegü 1251–58 | Restored centre + West Asia shock | Census, specialists, Baghdad | Last true dual-theatre qaghanate |
| Kublai Yuan 1271–79 | Sedentary conquest dynasty | Xiangyang package, naval/river war | Unity already fractured |

## 3. WEAKEST POINTS — Why weak

### 3.1 Succession crises — **Empire solvent #1**
**Why weak**
- Chinggisid succession by quriltai + senior-line politics repeatedly pauses armies (1227–29; 1241–46; 1248–51; **1259–64** civil war) (**CHR**).
- Personal union model: loyalty to qaghan/lineage more than to impersonal “Mongol state” (**MOD**).
- **Design:** Every qaghan death rolls a succession crisis wave; army freeze on distant maps; civil-war fork between brothers/cousins.

### 3.2 Japan 1274 & 1281 — **Naval / island limit**
**Why weak**
- Amphibious logistics, storms, Japanese defence, and coalition coordination failures (**CHR** Japanese/Chinese).
- Later “divine wind” (*kamikaze*) memory sacralises weather — use as **FOLK/memory overlay**, not sole historical cause (**MOD**).
- **Design:** Fleet supply meter; typhoon random (weighted by season); samurai shore towers; failure unlocks “limit of heaven’s mandate at sea” debuff.

### 3.3 Vietnam / Champa / tropical campaigns — **Terrain & attrition limit**
**Why weak**
- Heat, disease, guerrilla defence, riverine warfare blunt cavalry empire (**CHR** Đại Việt histories; **MOD**).
- Costly prestige expeditions without durable annexation.
- **Design:** Jungle attrition; elephant/guerrilla enemy packs; cavalry effectiveness floor.

### 3.4 ʿAyn Jālūt 1260 & Syrian ceiling — **Peer adaptation**
**Why weak**
- Mamluk military system answers Mongol tactics; Hülegü’s main force absent (**CHR/MOD**).
- Shows empire can be **stopped by prepared peers** when overextended and politically distracted.
- **Design:** Enemy “learn feigned retreat” tech; absence of main army = expeditionary brittle stack.

### 3.5 Overextension — **Map wider than glue**
**Why weak**
- Communications lag; local governors (*darughachi*) and subordinate khans acquire interests; simultaneous fronts (Song, Japan, Southeast Asia, ulūs wars) (**MOD**).
- Terror policy yields short-term surrender, long-term hatred coalitions and empty tax bases if overused (**CHR** rhetoric + **MOD** economic readings).
- **Design:** Corruption/distance meter; each new climate zone raises upkeep; atrocity slider cuts resistance now, growth later.

### 3.6 Settled-dynasty sinicization tensions (Yuan) — **Identity & elite fracture**
**Why weak**
- Mongol elite privileges vs Chinese bureaucratic norms; ethnic status hierarchies; court Buddhism vs Confucian elites (**CHR/MOD** Rossabi, Langlois).
- Military households and steppe recruitment dilute; rebellions find ethnic/political fuel (Red Turbans) (**CHR**).
- Parallel western story: Ilkhanid/Golden Horde **Islamisation** realigns diplomacy vs Mamluks/fellow Chinggisids (**MOD**).
- **Design:** “Steppe purity vs throne legitimacy” dual meter; choosing Chinese institutions buffs tax, nerfs tumen cohesion.

### 3.7 Ulūs wars (late 13th–14th c.) — **Chinggisid civil map**
**Why weak**
- Golden Horde vs Ilkhanate conflicts; Chagatai instability; Yuan claims of supremacy ignored (**CHR**).
- Fracture converts world-conquest engine into **regional dynasties** with Mongol branding.
- **Design:** After Act IV, player must pick one ulūs; former brothers become enemy AI with shared unit cosmetics.

### 3.8 Yuan fall 1368 — **Terminal China loss**
**Why weak**
- Fiscal strain, court faction, popular revolt, northern defence failure (**CHR/MOD**).
- Not “Mongols vanish” — Northern Yuan continues — but **China revenue engine** lost (**MOD**).
- **Design:** Capital siege finale; remnant steppe campaign unlock (hard mode, poor economy).

## 5. MYTHOLOGY / IDEOLOGY FOR ABILITIES — Tengri, ancestors, later court layers

> Do **not** paste modern neo-Tengrism, pan-Turkic revival aesthetics, or movie shaman spectacle onto 13th-c. Mongol courts as default dogma.  
> Separate **attested medieval practices & rhetoric** (Tengri / Heaven mandate language, ancestor cult, shamanic specialists, mountain/ongon rites in SHM and observers) from **FOLK** reconstructions.  
> Later **Buddhist** (especially Yuan–Tibetan) and **Islamic** (Ilkhanate, Golden Horde) layers are **historical court religions**, not “DLC pagan packs” — but they are **chronologically late relative to Chinggis’s generation**.

| Figure / complex | Portfolio (medieval sense) | Ability design hooks | Evidence split |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tengri (Tngri / Heaven)** | Sky/Heaven as sovereign power; qaghan ruled by Heaven’s will | Mandate meter; weather omen buffs; “Heaven favours the bold” morale | SHM + steppe rhetoric **CHR**; not monotheism clone (**MOD**); neo-Tengrism = **FOLK** |
| **Ancestor cult / Chinggisid sacral blood** | Charisma of the golden line; burial secrecy traditions | Bloodline legitimacy aura; forbidden tomb fog of war | SHM/Chinese notices **CHR**; tourist “tomb hunt” = **FOLK** |
| **Ongon / spirit helpers** | Household/clan spirit supports | Totem ward on camp keep; minor heal | Ethnographic + medieval hints **CHR/MOD**; systematised pantheon charts often late |
| **Shaman / böö specialists** | Divination, spirit negotiation, crisis ritual | Reveal fog; purge curse; risky trance cooldown | Observers (Rubruck etc.) **CHR**; Hollywood lightning shaman = **FOLK** |
| **Sacred mountains / Otgontenger-type awe** | Landscape holiness (regional) | Map hazard: offend mountain → storm debuff | Place cults **MOD/CHR** mix; do not invent single Olympus |
| **Sülder / martial fortune concepts** | War luck, spirit of victory (interpretive) | Streak buff after feigned retreat win | Conceptual **MOD**; careful not to over-systematise |
| **Tibetan Buddhism at Yuan court** | Phags-pa, imperial preceptor politics | Ritual shield on capital; diplomacy with temple towers | Yuan **CHR/DOC**; late relative to 1206 |
| **Islam in western ulūs** | Ilkhanid conversion arcs; Golden Horde Islamisation | Mosque-quarter tax; jihad enemy rhetoric on Mamluk maps | Persian/Arabic **CHR**; not Chinggis’s personal religion |
| **Nestorian Christian networks** | Kerait/Naiman elites; court presence | Envoy soft power; western diplomatic options | **CHR** (Rubruck, Syriac); minority prestige |
| **Manichaean / local syncretic residues** | Central Asian religious patchwork | Optional flavour shrines | Sparse; label carefully **MOD** |
| **Modern neo-Tengrism** | 20th–21st c. revival / identity politics | Cosmetic altar skin only | **FOLK** — never as “authentic 1220 unlock” |

### Anti-FOLK misuse checklist (mythology)
1. Chinggis as eternal Tengrist pope of Eurasia = **FOLK** oversimplification.  
2. Neo-Tengrist flags/symbols ≠ 13th-c. military standards.  
3. “Mongols hated all religions” = false; policy often **plural pragmatism** under Heaven rhetoric (**CHR/MOD**).  
4. Shaman boss who solo-kills cities with lightning = **FOLK**; specialists advise, armies win.  
5. Islam as eternal civilizational enemy = false after Ilkhanid/Golden Horde conversions.  
6. Tibetan Buddhism as “ancient Mongol core faith” = anachronism for 1206 founding act.

## 7. POWER CURVE SUMMARY + CAMPAIGN SPINE (TD acts)

### Strength curve (schematic)
Tribal warfare chaos → **1206 unification spike** → Xi Xia/Jin **siege-learning ramp** → Khwarezm **terror-conquest spike** → 1227 succession pause → Ögedei **institutional consolidation** → **1236–41 Europe operational max** → succession troughs → **Möngke reboot** → **1258 Baghdad shock** → **1260–64 fracture** → Kublai **Yuan China max** with western ulūs drift → **Japan/Vietnam limit troughs** → ulūs wars → **1368 China loss** (steppe remnant coda).

|---|---|---|---|---|

| **II** | *Khwarezm Storm* | Merchant casus belli; city terror/clemency; chase west | Atrocity vs tax base; overextended pursuit | Otrar → Kalka recon glory |

| **IV** | *Heaven’s Second Bow* | Möngke census; Hülegü to Baghdad | Dual-theatre strain; Mamluk answer forming | 1258 win / 1260 ʿAyn Jālūt warning |

| **VI** | *Ulūs Autumn* (epilogue) | Play one successor khanate; face brothers | Islamisation/Buddhism choices; Ming rising | Survive as Horde/Ilkhan/Chagatai *or* fall 1368 as Yuan |

**Optional parallel campaigns (do not merge into one eternal horde):**
- **Ilkhanate DLC:** Alamut → Baghdad → Syria limit → Islamisation politics.  
- **Golden Horde DLC:** Rus’ tribute tower network; Horde vs Ilkhan wars.  
- **Japan failure raid:** Dedicated amphibious act with typhoon system.  
- **Ming Rising reverse campaign:** Play Red Turban / Zhu Yuanzhang against Yuan keeps.

**Faction rule after 1260:** Yuan, Ilkhanate, Chagatai, Golden Horde = **separate tech trees** sharing horse-archer DNA and Chinggisid legitimacy cosmetics.

## 9. ASLA-STYLE “DO NOT ASSUME” CHECKLIST (15+)

1. **“The Mongols” ≠ one unchanging faction from 1206 to 1400.** United empire and successor ulūs differ.  
2. **Horde ≠ mindless barbarians.** Admin, census, law (*yasa* debates), specialists matter (**MOD**).  
3. **Secret History is not a camera feed** — epic political memory (**CHR**).  
4. **Army sizes in chronicles are not census truth.**  
5. **Japan failed only because of kamikaze** — incomplete; logistics/defence matter (**MOD**).  
6. **Neo-Tengrism ≠ medieval Mongol religion.** Label **FOLK**.  
7. **Chinggis was not a modern nationalist** or comic-book villain origin story.  
8. **All Mongols were not eternally Tengrist pagans** — Buddhism/Islam later reshape courts.  
9. **Baghdad 1258 ≠ eternal Mongol–Muslim cosmic war** — Ilkhanate later Muslim.  
10. **Europe was not permanently conquered in 1241** — operational raid-invasion then political stop.  
11. **Kublai’s Yuan is not “China became Mongolia” simply** — conquest dynasty with sinicizing pressures.  
12. **Cavalry solves jungles and navies** — Vietnam/Japan prove limits.  
13. **Subutai is not a solitary genius without institutions** — system + talent.  
14. **Yasa as a single written law code** — debated reconstruction (**MOD**); don’t invent a full legal PDF.  
15. **Marco Polo as default narrator** — optional, contested.  
16. **1368 ended all Mongol power** — Northern Yuan and other Chinggisid lines continue.  
17. **Terror was the only Mongol tactic** — surrender inducement + administration coexist.  
18. **Women were politically absent** — regents and lineage politics attested.  
19. **Golden Horde was only “Russian history’s dark age”** — fiscal-military system with steppe logic (**MOD**).  
20. **Siegecraft was foreign to Mongols forever** — they **adopted and scaled** it rapidly.  
21. **One tech tree named “Mongol” covering Yuan and Horde identically after 1260.**  
22. **Weather miracles as design win conditions without logistics meters.**  
23. **Modern horse-archer games = accurate decimal logistics.**  
24. **Equating Tangut Xi Xia, Jurchen Jin, and Song as one “Chinese enemy.”** Separate polities.

## 12. DESIGNER ONE-PAGE — STRONGEST VS WEAKEST

**Strongest when:** tribes are forcibly unified; decimal command holds; Chinese/Persian siege packages are absorbed; multi-column timing works (Mohi); census/admin feeds remounts; enemies misread feigned retreats; qaghan authority is uncontested.

**Weakest when:** qaghan dies and armies freeze; brothers fight (Toluid war); fleets meet typhoons and shore castles (Japan); jungles and elephants eat cavalry (Vietnam); Mamluks adapt (ʿAyn Jālūt); map distance breeds ulūs interests; Yuan sinicization splits elite incentives; Ming/Red Turban siege strips the China revenue engine (1368).

**Campaign spine reminder:** *Quriltai Forge* → *Khwarezm Storm* → *Thunder in the West* → *Heaven’s Second Bow* (Baghdad) → *Fracture & Yuan* (Japan fail branch) → *Ulūs Autumn* — never one eternal “horde king” fantasy after 1260.

## 13. QUICK REFERENCE — PEAKS / WEAKNESSES / ACTS (handoff)

### Peaks (WHY)
1. **United steppe war machine (1206–27, revived 1251–59):** decimal OS, mobility, intelligence, quriltai legitimacy.  
2. **Chinese siege-tech adoption (Jin → Song/Xiangyang):** artisans + trebuchets + river/naval learning → sedentary empires become solvable.

1. **Succession / civil war** — structural pause and fracture (esp. 1260–64).  
2. **Japan & Vietnam limits** — naval/tropical theatres deny horse-empire physics.  
3. **Overextension** — communications, empty tax bases, peer adaptation (Mamluks).  
4. **Settled-dynasty tensions** — Yuan sinicization & western Islamisation realign elites; ulūs wars; **1368** China loss.
