### Negative themes (relative frequency)

| Rank | Theme | Rel. freq. | Platform skew |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Aggressive IAP / content paywalls** (heroes, towers, maps) | **Very High** | Mobile App Store + Play dominate; “$50+ and half the map locked” |
| 2 | **Short campaign / thin launch content** | **Very High** | Steam: sub-~6h campaign, few bonus/endless maps vs prior entries |
| 3 | Tower/hero balance & experiment depth down | **High** | Dominant towers; artillery/CC feel weak; hero pool “uninspired” |
| 4 | Tower-disable / gold-gate boss design | **Med–High** | Steam: bosses destroy/disable towers → money is the real enemy |
| 5 | Price vs value (Steam sticker + mobile DLC stack) | **Med–High** | Steam “highest price / shortest game”; mobile “AAA total cost” |

### Design implications (KR5)

- **Ship a complete base strategy kit.** Locking alternate towers/heroes behind IAP collapses “many paths to win” into one viable free kit — a top mobile hate driver.
- **Launch content volume is a review score risk** even when core loop is loved. Bonus maps, challenges, encyclopedia, and endless/survival are table stakes for KR-like sequels.
- Dual heroes are a **praiseable differentiator** if micro stays optional and heroes are not win-condition OP.
- Prefer **enemy pressure and pathing puzzles** over disabling the player’s built towers / escalating repair gold — players frame that as anti-learning and anti-fun.
- Split monetization: **premium PC = full unlock**; mobile paid+IAP must not make the map look like an ad wall.

- Invest in **late-wave performance** — power users are your review amplifiers.
- Progressive unlock / Monkey Knowledge-style onboarding prevents option paralysis while feeding long-term goals.

- Challenge modes, score ghosts, and difficulty ladders multiply hours more cheaply than campaign length alone — but free map sharing multiplies further.
- Co-op needs matchmaking hygiene (dropouts, browsers); social modes fail loudly when empty.

## Design implications for our GDD (actionable)

1. **Monetization ethics are review score.** Mobile IAP that gates strategy options (KR5) or breaks session flow with ads (PvZ2) becomes the #1 complaint even when gameplay is loved. Prefer premium or cosmetic/battle-pass with full base roster.
2. **Launch with mode depth, not only campaign.** Endless/survival, challenges, puzzle, score attacks, and an almanac are expected. KR5’s thin extras and DG2’s UGC tax both hurt longevity perception.
3. **Protect player agency.** Do not regularly disable/sell-back towers mid-fight as a difficulty lever; punish with enemy design and pathing instead.
4. **Role clarity > roster size.** Distinct counter types (air, armor, swarm, boss) keep tower experiment fun; “spam the one that works” kills strategy reviews.
5. **Offline + stable sessions.** Paid players expect to play on planes; forced update gates and unclosable ads are disproportionate 1-star generators.
6. **Juice and identity scale praise.** Humor, readable art, soundtrack, and interactable maps show up as top praise across KR and PvZ — cheap retention vs new systems.
7. **Free UGC or don’t ship Workshop.** If community maps exist, free play must be first-class (DG2 is the cautionary tale).
8. **Hero micro is optional spice.** Dual/active heroes are praised when they deepen choices (KR5, BTD6 heroes) and hated when they replace tower strategy or become paywalled win-buttons.

## Appendix — Top 5 praise & Top 5 complaints (cross-title)

### Top 5 praise themes

1. **Deep, satisfying tower-defense strategy** that rewards mastery over dozens–hundreds of hours (BTD6, DG2, PvZ).
2. **Charming presentation** — humor, art, music, Easter eggs (KR5, PvZ; BTD6 personality).
3. **Fair premium / ad-light value** when the purchase includes the real game (BTD6, PvZ GOTY, KR5 PC).
4. **Long-tail content** — updates, challenge modes, endless/survival, co-op (BTD6; PvZ modes; DG2 challenges).
5. **Fresh mechanical twists that still feel like TD** — dual heroes (KR5), maze/core theft (DG2), plant/zombie fantasy (PvZ).

### Top 5 complaint themes

1. **Predatory or gated monetization** — hero/tower/map IAP (KR5 mobile), Workshop map fees (DG2), ad walls / ad-removal subs (PvZ 2).
2. **Not enough content at launch / short campaign** (KR5 Steam; DG2 vs DG1 expectations).
3. **Technical / access friction** — forced online updates (BTD6), crashes/lag, unclosable ads (PvZ 2).
4. **Shallow or skewed balance** — few viable towers, flying-heavy metas, tower-disable bosses (KR5, DG2).
5. **Sequel / franchise trust damage** — “worse than the classic,” greed vs predecessor goodwill (KR5, DG2, PvZ franchise discourse).
