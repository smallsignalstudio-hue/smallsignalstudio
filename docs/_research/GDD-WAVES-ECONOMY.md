# Dalga tasarımı, ekonomi, pacing

## 1. Seviye uzunluğu hedefleri

| Mod | Süre | Wave sayısı |
|-----|------|-------------|
| Tutorial | 3–5 dk | 5–8 |
| Standard campaign | 8–14 dk | 12–20 |
| Boss siege | 12–18 dk | 15 + phases |
| Daily challenge | 6–10 dk | 10–14 |
| Endless / Survival | uncapped | escalating |

## 2. Wave composition grammar

Her wave bir **cümle**:
`[opener runners] + [main force] + [special] + [optional bosslet]`

### Escalation curve
- W1–3: teach (single type).
- W4–7: mix armor.
- W8–12: siege engines appear.
- Mid: mini-boss.
- Final: general + mixed army + scripted event (gate break / fire).

### Breed / send pressure (BTD-inspired, historical flavor)
- “Reinforcement horn” — enemy can spawn mid-path from boats/tunnels (Japan/China siege).
- Announce 2s before for fairness.

## 3. Gold economy

### Sources
- Starting gold (map script).
- Per kill (scaled by enemy value).
- Wave clear bonus.
- Early-call bonus (diminishing).
- Interest? (optional hard maps — BTD banker vibe with “merchant caravan” support tower).

### Sinks
- Tower place/upgrade.
- Hero ability reagents (rare).
- Barricade consumables.
- Repair keep (expensive, discouraged).

### Starting gold philosophy
- Enough for 2–3 base towers; not full board.
- Forced early decisions = engagement.

## 4. Early call

- Button: call next wave early.
- Reward: +X% wave gold + small seal chance on perfect.
- Risk: overlapping waves if mis-timed.
- Kingdom Rush DNA — **must have**.

## 5. Star rating

- ★1: clear
- ★2: keep HP ≥ 70% OR no leak past inner ward
- ★3: keep HP ≥ 90% + speed clear OR no sell abuse

Heroic/Iron separate medals → **Codex seals**.

## 6. Meta progression (out of run)

- Commander level (small global QoL, NOT raw damage %).
- Tower mastery XP (cosmetic titles + tiny range/xp — cap carefully).
- Civ affinity (unlock voice lines / alt skins).
- Account collection % for completionists.

**Anti-P2W:** meta bonuses soft-capped; hard mode disables most meta.

## 7. Fail / retry

- Defeat screen: tip + rewarded “retry with bonus”.
- Checkpoint every 5 waves on long sieges (optional accessibility).
- No energy to retry.

## 8. Pacing juice beats

- Wave horn SFX.
- Mid-siege scripted “ram hits gate” camera shake.
- Victory banner with civ language flavor.
- Defeat: keep cracks, not shame ads.
